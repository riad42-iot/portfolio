About Me

Who I Am

A passionate engineer on a mission to build technology that matters

IoT & Robotics Engineer

I am an IoT and Robotics Engineering student at the University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB). My journey started with a rough first year—but I turned failure into fuel. I went on to win the Dean's Award, rank 1st in my batch in 2nd year, and publish 1 research papers in IEEE conferences.

I've built a sub-$250 AI telepresence medical robot, a secure military mesh communication system, and I've been selected for the National Round of Robo Fusion 1.0 in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Recently, I won the Gold Award at WSDG 2026 for my military mesh project. My goal: use technology to save lives and bridge the gap between rural communities and quality healthcare.
