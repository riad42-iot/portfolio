## 📖 About This Portfolio

This is my **personal portfolio website** — a modern, interactive, and fully responsive showcase of my work as an **IoT and Robotics Engineer**. It reflects my journey from a challenging first year to winning the **Dean's Award**, publishing **4 IEEE research papers**, and representing Bangladesh at the **Robo Fusion 1.0 National Round** in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

The portfolio is designed to be more than just a CV — it's a **visual story** of resilience, innovation, and the relentless pursuit of building technology that matters.

> 🚀 **Built by:** Md. Al-Riad  
> 🏛️ **University:** University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB)  
> 🎯 **Mission:** Use technology to save lives and bridge healthcare gaps in developing nations.
